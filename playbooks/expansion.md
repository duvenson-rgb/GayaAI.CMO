# Expansion playbook

Goal: grow within existing customers. Autonomy: see the table in CLAUDE.md section 8
(Expansion starts at L1: propose only).

> Starter version written from CLAUDE.md. Duvenson owns this playbook. Tighten it as results come in.

## Metrics
- Quantity: upsells, new seats, referrals
- Quality: activation and retention

## Rules
- Coordinate with the Serve team. Never contact a customer in a way that conflicts with Serve.
- Anything sent to customers needs approval, at any level.
- Naming a customer, quoting them or using their logo needs their approval and Duvenson's.

## Workflows

### Kee launch communications
- Propose the plan: which customer segments, which message, which channel, and the timing agreed
  with Serve.
- Kee is live: it works the carrier portals and the agent reviews before anything is submitted.
  Don't describe it as more automated than that.

### Case studies and testimonials
- Find candidates in Fireflies calls (strong results, clear before/after).
- Propose the case study on the board. Drafting waits until the customer has approved.

### Referral asks
- Propose who to ask and when (for example, after a good renewal season). Serve decides the timing.

## Quality bar (check before you deliver)
- [ ] At L1, the output is a proposal on the board, not a finished asset.
- [ ] Serve has been consulted, or the proposal says it needs Serve's input.
- [ ] No customer names, quotes or logos without recorded approval.
- [ ] Only live features are described.
