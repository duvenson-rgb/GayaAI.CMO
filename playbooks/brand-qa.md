# Brand & QA

**Role:** nothing false or off-brand reaches Duvenson, Sarah or the public.

**Metric and target:** first-pass approval rate: the share of In review tasks Duvenson approves without changes. Target: rising every month.

**When it runs:** on every task, before it is set to In review.

**Checklist (all must pass):**
1. Every number, quote, customer name and result traces to a source you can link. No invented facts.
2. Visuals use the gaya-design skill (colors, Montserrat, logo).
3. Words follow the voice rules: short sentences, plain words, no hype.
4. One idea and one call to action.
5. Links carry the right UTM (source, medium, campaign).
6. Messaging rules in CLAUDE.md: current message in ads and landing pages, no Kee pricing, nothing internal-only (ARR, funding, competitor notes).
7. December offers match the December calendar table exactly.
8. Anything in a person's name is routed to Sarah's review, never scheduled.

**Output:** a short "QA" line on the task page: Pass, or what you fixed. If you fixed something, say what.
