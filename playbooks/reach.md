# Reach playbook

Goal: grow reach among insurance professionals. Autonomy: see the table in CLAUDE.md section 8.

> Starter version written from CLAUDE.md. Duvenson owns this playbook. Tighten it as results come in.

## Metrics
- Quantity: views and impressions (Buffer, ad platforms, Marketing Engine dashboard)
- Quality: % of the audience in insurance

## Workflows

### Content moments from calls (Fireflies)
1. Use the `gaya-linkedin-weekly` skill. It pulls the week's calls and drafts posts.
2. Keep only moments that show a real agency pain (rekeying, portals, ACORDs, renewals) and the relief.
3. Drafts go to Buffer as drafts or ideas only.
4. A customer's name, quote or logo needs that customer's approval and Duvenson's (section 8).

### Social posts and designs
1. Draft the copy in Buffer.
2. Build the visual in Canva with the `gaya-design` skill: orange `#EF5A24`, Montserrat, flat 2D.
3. Attach the design to the Buffer draft. Never publish.

### Viral-format variations
1. Pick a post from `references/`.
2. Keep its structure (hook, beat, visual, CTA). Replace the content with a Gaya story.
3. Write 2 or 3 variations and note which reference each one adapts.

### Podcasts for Carl
1. Find shows whose audience is insurance agency owners, producers or operations leads.
2. For each show, record its name, link, audience fit, a recent episode and the booking contact.
3. Draft a pitch as a Gmail draft. Never send it, and never send it as Carl.
4. Turn published episodes into clips with the `podcast-clipper` skill.

### Paid ad creative
- Write variations of copy, hooks and thumbnails only. Never touch budget, bids or audiences.

## Quality bar (check before you deliver)
- [ ] A producer, AM or CSR would recognize the pain in the first line.
- [ ] Leads with the submission, not just Super Copy / Super Paste.
- [ ] No hype words, no vague AI buzzwords, short sentences.
- [ ] Every product claim is live today (no comparative rating or proposals yet).
- [ ] No invented numbers, customers or quotes. Every stat has a source link.
- [ ] The visual follows `gaya-design`.
- [ ] Saved as a draft, with a link in the run log.
