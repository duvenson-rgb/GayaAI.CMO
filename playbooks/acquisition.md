# Acquisition playbook

Goal: turn reach into qualified contacts. Autonomy: see the table in CLAUDE.md section 8.

> Starter version written from CLAUDE.md. Duvenson owns this playbook. Tighten it as results come in.

## Metrics
- Quantity: new marketing contacts in Attio
- Quality: % that pass the qualified-lead rubric (CLAUDE.md section 3), plus their total estimated premium

## Workflows

### Weekly event sweep
1. Look for US insurance events in the next 6 months: state agent associations, carrier and
   network conferences, E&S, trucking, high net worth and coastal events.
2. Add each one to the Notion events board with its date, location, audience, cost and link.
   Check the board first so you don't add duplicates.
3. For events that fit, draft the landing page, the registration form and a pre-event plan.
   Attending, sponsoring or paying for anything needs approval.

### Webinar operations
- For each webinar, draft the page, the registration form, reminders (7 days, 1 day, 1 hour) and
  a follow-up sequence (attended / missed).
- Emails stay as Gmail drafts. Pages are drafts until Duvenson approves them.

### Lead magnets
- ROI calculator, guides, checklists. ROI numbers and product claims need approval.
- Every lead magnet has a form that records the lead source in Attio.

### Enrich and score new contacts
1. Find marketing-sourced contacts added since the last scoring run.
2. Fill in organization type, estimated written premium, role and source.
3. Set the rubric field to Pass / Fail / Unknown. Unknown is not Pass.
4. Add an Attio note with evidence: a link and one line per test (Fit, Role, Reachable).
5. Never change deal stages or sales-owned fields.

## Quality bar (check before you deliver)
- [ ] Every scored contact has evidence for all three tests.
- [ ] Premium estimates name their source. If the premium can't be found, the rubric is Unknown.
- [ ] Events target US P&C professionals, not general insurtech crowds.
- [ ] Every form captures the lead source.
- [ ] Nothing was published, sent or paid for without approval.
