# Cause families by domain

Pick the set that fits the problem, then adapt: rename, merge or add a family when the domain calls for it. Use 4–7 families. Each family lists prompting questions to drive the brainstorm in Step 3.

## Contents
- 6M — operations, manufacturing, any physical process
- SaaS / go-to-market (sales & marketing pipeline)
- Customer success / retention / adoption
- Marketing 8P

## 6M — operations, manufacturing, physical processes

| Family | FR | Prompting questions |
|---|---|---|
| Method | Méthode | Is there a defined procedure? Is it followed? Is it the right one? Did it change recently? |
| Materials | Matière | Are inputs (raw materials, data, information) complete, correct, on time? Did a supplier change? |
| Manpower | Main-d'œuvre | Are people trained, staffed, motivated? Is knowledge concentrated in one person? |
| Machines | Matériel | Are tools, equipment, software reliable and adequate? Maintenance? Recent upgrades? |
| Environment | Milieu | Workplace conditions, seasonality, regulation, market, organizational context? |
| Measurement | Mesure | Is the problem measured correctly? Are the right indicators tracked? Would we notice drift early? |

## SaaS / go-to-market (pipeline, conversion, revenue problems)

| Family | Prompting questions |
|---|---|
| ICP & Market | Are we targeting the right segment? Did the market or buyer change? Wrong-fit leads? |
| Message & Offer | Is the value clear and differentiated? Pricing, packaging, proof (case studies, ROI)? |
| Channels | Which channels bring leads? Volume vs quality? Did a channel degrade (deliverability, ad costs, algorithm)? |
| Sales Process | Stages, follow-up speed, demo quality, handoffs, objection handling, next steps? |
| People | Rep skills, ramp, capacity, incentives, ownership? |
| Tools & Data | CRM hygiene, enrichment, automations, attribution, broken integrations? |
| Product | Does the product deliver what's promised in the sales cycle? Gaps prospects hit in trials? |
| Measurement | Are funnel stages defined consistently? Are we looking at the right conversion metric? |

## Customer success / retention / adoption

| Family | Prompting questions |
|---|---|
| Onboarding | Time to first value, setup friction, who's involved, training quality? |
| Product Fit | Was this the right customer? Do their workflows match what the product does? |
| Product Quality | Bugs, performance, missing integrations, reliability? |
| Customer Context | Their seasonality, staffing, champion turnover, competing priorities, internal buy-in? |
| Relationship | Ownership on our side, check-in cadence, responsiveness, escalation paths? |
| Value Proof | Do they see the ROI? Do we show it to them? Usage reporting? |
| Measurement | Is "active" defined? Do we have leading indicators of churn? |

## Marketing 8P

Product, Price, Place (distribution), Promotion, People, Process, Physical evidence (proof, brand touchpoints), Performance (metrics). Useful for campaign or brand problems.

## Service 4S

Surroundings (environment, context), Suppliers (partners, vendors), Systems (processes, tools), Skills (people's capabilities). Lightweight set for service delivery problems.

## Software incidents & engineering

| Family | Prompting questions |
|---|---|
| Code | Recent changes, edge cases, untested paths? |
| Infrastructure | Capacity, configuration, environment differences? |
| Dependencies | Third-party APIs, libraries, upstream services, data sources? |
| Process | Review, testing, deployment, rollback practices? |
| People & Knowledge | Documentation, ownership, on-call readiness, bus factor? |
| Monitoring | Was it detectable? How long until someone noticed? Alert quality? |

## Personal / team productivity

| Family | Prompting questions |
|---|---|
| Method | How is work planned and prioritized? Is there a system? |
| Commitments | Too many projects? Unclear priorities? Saying yes too often? |
| Environment | Interruptions, workspace, meetings, time zones? |
| Tools | Do tools help or fragment attention? |
| Skills | Missing skills that slow things down? Delegation? |
| Energy | Schedule vs energy peaks, sleep, breaks? |
| Measurement | Is progress visible? Are goals defined? |
