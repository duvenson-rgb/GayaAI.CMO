# Gaya guardrails (check every draft against these)

## 1. Messaging
- Gaya is a **quoting automation tool** for independent insurance agencies. Never call it an
  "AI copilot" (even though some older brand files do).
- Super Copy, Super Paste and Super Clipboard are proper nouns.
- Voice: confident, plain, benefit-first, speaks to you. No emoji, no exclamation spam, sentence case.

## 2. Numbers: approved proof points only
- **450+ agencies** (never 250+, 300+, 350+)
- **3–10× faster quotes**
- **15–20 minutes saved per quote**
- **SOC 2 Type II**
- **Personal and Commercial Lines**
Not allowed until confirmed: "500+ carriers" / "550 carriers" (heard on sales calls, unverified),
"3–4× faster", "80%", "0 copy-paste errors", "Trusted by 1,000s of agents".
Numbers a customer says about their own agency can be used as their quote, with permission.
Any other number → flag "Needs fact check".

## 3. Product truth
Describe only what the product does:
- Super Copy captures data from **mapped, editable** pages on supported platforms. It doesn't read
  PDFs; documents go in by upload/extraction (dec pages, handwritten notes, photos, ACORDs, notes typed
  in plain language).
- Super Paste fills fields **one after another**, only where it has data, and marks each pasted field
  with the Gaya G. Defaults paste with a **black G**. It doesn't invent missing values.
- The agent stays on the page. Gaya isn't an autonomous agent that logs into carriers.
- Data reaches the portal when someone saves the record; it isn't automatic sync.
- No success toast/banner after copy or paste. Nothing fills "all at once".
- Integrations (Salt, HawkSoft, AMS360, Momentum, Zillow "Send to Gaya") can be mentioned only as shown
  on calls; don't expand their scope. Partner integrations need partner approval before announcement.
- Visuals: real footage from a staging build only. When a pasted field is shown, only the Gaya G logo
  inside the box on the right side, no circle/ring/badge (unless told the product changed).
  Super Copy = purple, Super Paste = green.

## 4. People and permissions
- Customer name, agency name or quote → **Needs customer permission**. Until then, write an
  anonymized version ("an agency in Oklahoma", "a CSR at a personal-lines shop") and say so.
- Partner names (Momentum, Leavitt, Salt, TWFG…) → **Needs partner approval**.
- Posts in a founder's or teammate's voice are drafts for them to approve and edit. Never invent their
  opinions, feelings or experiences; build only on what they said on calls. Mark inferred opinions.
- Carrier names (Progressive, GEICO, National General…) are fine as facts about a workflow; never
  imply a partnership or endorsement.

## 5. Never publish
Pricing or contract talk, low-usage or churn signals, hiring/comp, personal or health details, jokes
from pre-call banter, demo failures, criticism of a named vendor.

## 6. Pre-ship checklist (run on every draft)
- [ ] Hook pays off in the body
- [ ] Every fact traces to a linked moment or an approved proof point
- [ ] No unapproved numbers; no "AI copilot"
- [ ] Product behavior described accurately (section 3)
- [ ] Names anonymized unless permission is confirmed
- [ ] Author voice matches voices.md; nothing put in their mouth they didn't say
- [ ] Humanizer rules: no hype, AI tells, hashtags, emojis, semicolons
- [ ] CTA matches funnel stage
- [ ] Media suggestion uses real footage/photos only
