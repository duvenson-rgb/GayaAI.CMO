# Visual decision: photo, video, screenshot or design?

Design is the fallback, not the default. A real face or real footage beats a graphic when one
exists. Decide per draft (A and B can differ), then record the decision in the draft.

## Decision order (first match wins)

1. **VIDEO clip**: the motion is the proof.
   A live reaction ("that is so cool"), fields filling on a real form, a workaround being done.
   - Source: cut 10–30 s from the Fireflies recording (do it during the run; Fireflies media
     links expire in a few days), or a fresh staging screen recording for product-only footage.
   - Customer on screen, or customer data on screen → blur data, crop faces not asked, and mark
     "Needs customer permission". If the only footage shows real customer data, prefer a staging
     recording of the same workflow.

2. **PHOTO of the author**: the post is personal or in someone's voice.
   Team stories, day-in-the-life, founder opinions, lessons learned.
   - Best: a selfie or candid photo the author takes (on a call, at their desk, with the team).
     The draft tells them exactly what to shoot, e.g. "selfie on an onboarding call, laptop visible".
   - Fallback: a frame from a Fireflies call where the author is alone on screen (speaker view).
     Crop out other participants and the Fireflies tile. Author must approve.

3. **SCREENSHOT of the real product**: one screen explains the point.
   A feature (defaults with the black G, pasted fields with the G), before/after on a form.
   - Staging build with demo data, or real screen with data blurred. Never drawn UI presented as real.

4. **DESIGNED graphic**: the content is a structure that stands on its own.
   Frameworks, numbered steps, questions, lists, approved stats, or a quote card when there's no
   permission to show the person or the footage. Company-page posts use this most.
   - Follow the gaya-design skill and the uploaded social examples (logo top, Montserrat headline
     with one orange phrase, one supporting line, proof points in wide caps).
   - Vary the designs within a week: don't reuse one surface/layout for every card. Rotate surfaces
     (paper, ink, solid orange, purple stage, warm gradient for quotes only) and layouts (flow diagram,
     quote card, before/after ticket, timeline, 2x2 grid, big statement) to fit each draft's structure.

5. **Text only**: short hot takes where any visual would be decoration. Use rarely.

## Rules of thumb
- Story drafts → usually photo or video. Framework drafts → usually design or screenshot.
- Author is a person (founder, teammate) → start from a photo of them; add design only if the
  structure needs it.
- Never invent a stat or UI to fill a design.
- Media hierarchy (LinkedIn): photo with a face > team photo > candid photo > infographic > carousel
  > screenshot > no media. Video of a real moment sits with photos at the top.

## What to record under each draft
- **Media type**: Video / Photo / Screenshot / Design / Text only
- **Why**: one line
- **Source or brief**: Fireflies timestamp + clip in/out, what the author should shoot, or the
  design brief
- **Who acts**: "I made it" (design, clip, frame) or "Author: take a selfie on a call"
- **Consent**: none / author / customer / partner
- **Fallback**: the next option down the list
